My Story

Most marketing goes wrong long before the first ad goes live — in the assumptions nobody questioned and the tracking nobody checked.

That's where I start. Whether it's a Meta account that's been scaling the wrong campaign for months, a Google Ads budget quietly leaking into broad-match searches, or a YouTube channel publishing good videos nobody can find — the fix usually begins with an honest audit rather than a bigger budget.

I work across YouTube Promotion, Google Ads, Music Promotion, YouTube SEO, Social Media Marketing and Creative & Content Support. Some clients need one of those; most need two or three working together. I'd rather tell you which one matters right now than sell you a bundle you don't need.
